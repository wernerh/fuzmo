One body, seven personalities

Archetypes come from a week of behaviour. They change the face, the antenna and the props, never the silhouette.

Sleepy

Lots of night use. Yawns mid-sentence.

Gremlin

Hops between apps. Steals its own snacks.

Drama Queen

Big daily swings. Faints at minor setbacks.

Genius

Reads and focuses. Corrects your grammar.

Zombie

Three cooked days in a row. Bulb dark.

Unhinged

Late nights plus chaos. Screams at nothing.

Legendary

30 balanced days plus luck. Can't be bought.
