Meet Fuzmo

A smooth purple gumdrop with one job: show you how cooked your brain is, without a single stat bar.

  1. 1
    Brain bulbThe brainrot meter. Bright and upright when you're sharp; it droops, dims and finally cracks as you scroll.
  2. 2
    Mismatched eyesThe left one is a little bigger. It makes him his, even as a 24 px icon.
  3. 3
    Gumdrop body, no furSquash-and-stretch comedy that is cheap to animate in Rive and stays crisp at any size.
  4. 4
    Lilac bellyThe only second colour. Moods tint the room, never Fuzmo himself.
  5. 5
    Stubby feetFor waddling, fainting and dramatic collapses onto the rug.
