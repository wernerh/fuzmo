# Score the night, not the whole day

Fuzmo scores one night at a time from phone-down time, wake-ups and late scrolling, instead of an hourly all-day "brainrot" score weighted by app category. Research showed the pain adults feel is the late-night scroll, that users distrust numbers they cannot check, and that iOS competitors already own the all-day mechanic. Use before the bedtime target is never counted.

## Considered Options

- Hourly all-day brainrot score with category weights (rejected: harder to explain, crowded market)
- Sleep-stage tracking via Health Connect or wearables (rejected: Play health declaration, hardware dependency)
